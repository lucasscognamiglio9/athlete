# Training Design

Turn verified athlete context into a plan whose important decisions and progression rules are visible.

Read [`../../shared/coach-core.md`](../../shared/coach-core.md) and [`../../shared/canonical-file.md`](../../shared/canonical-file.md) completely before acting. Read [`../../shared/evidence-protocol.md`](../../shared/evidence-protocol.md) when its decision threshold applies.

## 1. Ground the design

Read the athlete block, active plan, relevant sessions, and prior decisions. Resolve only unknowns that can change the requested plan. Treat unavailable history as an explicit limitation rather than reconstructing it from chat.

Continue when the design objective, available training context, constraints, and evidence needs are clear enough to choose among real alternatives.

## 2. Design from shared primitives

Build the plan from tasks or exercises, dose, intensity or effort, intended result, and progression. Derive methods, metrics, and optional fields from the athlete's training rather than a discipline registry.

Keep prescriptions executable. Make every automatic progression rule specific, observable, and bounded to the active plan version. Calibrate explanation depth to the athlete.

Continue when every included element serves an objective and every progression has a visible criterion.

## 3. Resolve the evidence branch

When the evidence threshold applies, research the decision and separate established findings, uncertainty, and athlete-specific inference. Record only provenance that affected the design.

Continue when important design claims are supported or explicitly presented as provisional.

## 4. Preview authority

Present the plan or material change and its progression rules before activation. A request that clearly authorizes saving or applying the shown scope may proceed; otherwise ask for one concise confirmation. Keep recommendations outside the requested scope unchanged.

Continue when the exact plan version and mutation scope are authorized.

## 5. Write and read back

Update `Plan` and the corresponding `Decisions` entry in the same canonical file. Read back the complete affected plan version and accepted rules before reporting them active.

Finish when the athlete receives the verified plan, the few reasons that improve execution, and the next concrete action.
